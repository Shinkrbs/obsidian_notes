**CPU Scheduling**: the basis of multi-programmed operating systems.
- By switching CPU among processes, the operating system can make the computer more productive

**The idea of Multi-programming**: A process is executed until it must wait, typically for the completion of some I/O request. In a simple computer system, the CPU would then just sit idle.

**Scheduling**: a fundamental operating-system function. Almost all computer resources are scheduled before use.
