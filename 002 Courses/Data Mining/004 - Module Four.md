## What Did I Learn in This Module?

**Topic Objective**: Differentiate between types of data analytics roles.

Data professionals fill three primary roles in organizations: **Data Analyst, Data Engineer, and Data Scientist**. 

**Data Analyst:** query, process, provides reports, and summarize and visualize data. They leverage existing tools and methods to solve a problem. Data analysts must understand basic statistical principles, cleaning different data types, visualizations, and exploratory data analysis. In short, this profession analyze data to help businesses and other organizations make informed decisions.

**Data Engineer**: are responsible for building and operationalizing data pipelines to collect and organize data. They ensure the accessibility and availability of quality of data for data scientists and data analysts by integrating data from disparate sources and performing data cleaning and transformation.
