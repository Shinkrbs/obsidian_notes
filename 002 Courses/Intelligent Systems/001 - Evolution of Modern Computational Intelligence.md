# Introduction to Intelligent Systems

**Intelligence**: a system's ability to measure, reason, perceive relationships and analogies, learn from experience, store and retrieve knowledge, solve problems, understand complex concepts, identify, generalize, and adjust to new circumstances.

**Human vs Machine Intelligence**
*Human*:
- Perceive by patterns
- Store and recall information by patterns
- Figure out full object even though some part of it is missing or distorted
*Machine*:
- Perceive by set of rules and data
- Store and recall information by searching algorithms
- Cannot determine precisely if some part of object is missing

**Intelligent Systems**
- a discipline that examines intelligent behaviors and their implementations as well as their influences on human society
- It is a framework based on the Artificial Intelligence approaches and techniques to carry out more precise and efficient operations to solve related problems.
