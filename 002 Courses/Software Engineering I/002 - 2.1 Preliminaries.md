**Client** -  provides the requirements and resources and expects a software product in return.

**Satisfaction** - the primary measurement of success.

*Generalizations:*
1. The effectiveness of the team determines the success.
2. Large software is rarely built from scratch.
3. Big projects involves large teams and ever-changing requirements.

**Software is Expensive**:
1. The major cost of any big projects is salary.
	1. Every software project has a tradeoff with:
			- Functionality
			- Cost and other resources
			- Time and Delivery
2. Managers oversee potential risks:
	1. Going over budget.
	2. Software Defects
	3. Late Delivery

**Software as a Product**:
1. Software is based on the client's needs.
